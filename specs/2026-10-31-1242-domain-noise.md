# vnorm — Domain Noise for `rvnorm()`: Design and Validation Log

**Purpose:** Record of `rvnorm(noise = "domain")`: the model, the design decisions,
the review that changed two of them, and the validation.

## 1. The model

Both existing variety normal versions put the noise in the **codomain** of g:
the kernel is a normal density on g/|∇g| (`homo = TRUE`) or on g. The new option
puts it in the **domain**:

  X = U + ε,  U ≈ uniform on the variety V (within the window `w`, if any),
  ε ~ N(0, Σ) on the variables in `vars`.

David's simulation scheme:
1. Draw from the homogeneous variety normal with a scalar *pilot* sd σₚ.
2. Project each draw onto V.
3. Add N(0, Σ) noise.

**Why the homogeneous version.** g/|∇g| is the signed distance to first order, so
the pilot's integral along each normal line is the same everywhere, and the
projected draws are uniform on V up to O(σₚ²) terms. The `homo = FALSE` version
would weight each point by 1/|∇g|. For systems, J⁺g plays the same role in the
normal space.

## 2. API (David's decisions)

- `noise = c("codomain", "domain")`. The default, `"codomain"`, changes nothing.
- `pilot_sd`, defaulting to a tenth of the noise scale, sqrt(mean(diag(Σ))) / 10.
- `sd` and `Sigma` give Σ for both single polynomials and systems:
  - `sd`: a scalar, or one value per variable;
  - `Sigma`: a scalar variance, a vector of variances, or a matrix;
  - order: sorted variable order, unless entries are named.
- Output:
  - `"tibble"` adds the latent points as `<var>_proj` columns, plus
    `.chain`, `.iteration` and `.draw`;
  - `"stanfit"` is an error, as are `homo = FALSE` and `inc_warmup = TRUE`;
  - `code_only` returns the pilot's program.
- `seed` also seeds the noise, under a local RNG state. Without it,
  `set.seed()` controls everything.

## 3. Changes from the approved plan, after review

An independent design review raised two points that I verified before acting on
them.

1. **Projection: Gauss–Newton, then the closest point.** Gauss–Newton
   (x ← x − J⁺g) lands O(d²) along the variety from the closest point, by an
   amount that depends on how g is written.
   - On the ellipse x²/4 + y² = 1, quadrature puts the base density's maximum
     deviation from uniform at:

     | σₚ | 0.1 | 0.05 | 0.02 | 0.01 |
     |---|---|---|---|---|
     | closest point | 1.08% | 0.27% | 0.044% | 0.011% |
     | Gauss–Newton | 3.19% | 0.80% | 0.127% | 0.032% |

   - `project_rows_closest()` (internal, R/project_onto_variety.R) therefore
     runs Gauss–Newton and then alternates tangent steps toward the start
     with Gauss–Newton, until x − u is normal to V.
   - It is still fast and still handles systems:
     - one polynomial: 0.034 s per 1,000 points, vectorized; it matches
       `project_onto_variety()` to 3e-10;
     - systems: 0.41 s per 1,000 points.
2. **Windows.** A window that cuts the pilot's tube thins U at its edges by
   O(σₚ). The pilot therefore runs on the window widened by 6σₚ, in the
   polynomial's variables, and latent points outside the real window are
   dropped. A scalar window stays scalar, so the templates still apply.

Other review points adopted:
- Survivors are subsampled evenly, so each chain keeps its share of draws.
- Top-up runs use `seed + round - 1`.
- A warning fires when more than 1% of projections fail.
- `Sigma` must be symmetric, since `chol()` reads only its upper triangle.

Not adopted: the review's fixed `pilot_sd = 0.02`. David chose noise sd / 10.
The review's concern stands, though: for very small noise, this costs time for
no accuracy gain.

Found along the way, and committed separately:
- **`d327379`.** The `seed` docs said `set.seed()` does not control Stan. In
  fact, cmdstanr draws a missing seed from R's RNG.
- **`44dbf33`.** R CMD check on the feature failed an existing test, "rvnorm()
  respects RNG seeds", because a chain's CSV had disappeared.
  - **Cause.** cmdstanr names output files after the minute and a
    `stats::runif()` draw. A fit's finalizer deletes its temp files. So two
    runs after the same `set.seed()`, in the same minute, share file names,
    and garbage collecting the first fit deletes the second's.
  - **Demonstration.** Two runs after `set.seed(1)` had identical names, and
    `rm(f1); gc()` removed f2's CSV.
  - **Exposure.** Users who follow the `set.seed()` advice hit this. So does
    domain mode's `seed`, which resets R's RNG on every call.
  - **Fix.** `rvnorm()` now passes cmdstanr an `output_basename` from
    `tempfile()`, unless one comes through `...`. `tempfile()` names do not
    depend on R's RNG.

## 4. Validation

All runs used an installed copy of the final tree, in fresh R processes; the
default HMC pilot ran on the shipped templates (`2_2_vn`).

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Ellipse, Σ = [.01 .006; .006 .01], 8,000 draws, rejection pilot (iid) | latent points on V; share with \|x\| > 1.5 is 0.349 (exact 0.359; uniform-in-angle would give 0.460); KS of arclength p = 0.30; cov(X − U) / Σ = 0.99, 0.96, 0.98 |
| Same, HMC pilot, 8 chains | share 0.345; arclength ESS 1,725 / 8,000; no divergences or max-treedepth hits; chains' shares 0.32–0.36. Raw KS rejects because of autocorrelation; with `thin = 25`, KS p = 0.74 |
| System (x² + y² + z² − 1, z), Σ = diag(.01, .02, .005) | z_proj = 0, radius 1 to 2e-16; KS of the angle p = 0.18; covariance ratios 1.01, 1.01, 0.99 |
| `x y - 1`, `w = 2` | rejection pilot: U inside the box, branches 0.50 / 0.50. HMC: 0.75 / 0.25, the documented disconnected-component caveat |
| Extra variable: `x^2 - 1`, `vars = c("x", "z")`, `w = 2` | x_proj = ±1 exactly, z_proj within (−2, 2); KS p = 0.002 on autocorrelated draws (z mixes slowly at σₚ = 0.005) |
| Domain vs codomain, sd 0.02, rejection draws | KS of \|g\|/\|∇g\| at X: p = 0.52; the models agree for small isotropic noise |

## 6. Out of scope and open

- `pdvnorm()` for the domain model. A Monte Carlo version could average
  φ_Σ(x − Uⱼ) over latent draws.
- A non-symmetric pilot window falls back to generated Stan code. That is
  correct, but slower than the templates.
